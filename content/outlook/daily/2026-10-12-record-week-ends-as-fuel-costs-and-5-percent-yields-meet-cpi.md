## Top of mind

U.S. stocks closed last week higher: on Friday, October 9, the S&P 500 rose about 0.6% to 7,811.54, just shy of its record, and the Dow gained about 0.8% to 51,654.95. The 10-year Treasury yield sits near 5.24% and Brent crude settled around $104.72, so the pressure is on costs and rates, not growth, and Delta's guidance cut on jet fuel is the clearest example. Bank earnings start Tuesday and September CPI lands Wednesday, with a market near records and little room for a hot print.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. Stocks cap a record-setting week
**Source:** AP (via La Nacion and DTN), CNBC
**So what:** Two independent recaps agree on direction and levels: the S&P 500 and Nasdaq set records earlier in the week and Friday's gains left them near highs after Thursday's AI-linked selloff. The market is priced for a clean earnings season, which raises the bar for every report.

### 2. Delta misses and cuts 2026 guidance on fuel
**Source:** CNBC, AP via DTN, reporting compiled by Stocktwits and Investinglive
**So what:** Adjusted EPS of $1.72 reportedly missed an LSEG consensus of $1.75, Delta's first miss in two years, and full-year EPS guidance fell to $5.10 to $5.60 from $6.50 to $7.50. Management blamed jet fuel and said demand stays strong. The share reaction conflicts: CNBC has Delta down about 4%, AP says it ended up less than 0.1%, so treat the close as unconfirmed.

### 3. Yields stay near multi-decade highs
**Source:** AP via DTN, Kiplinger
**So what:** A 10-year near 5.24% and a 2-year near 4.79% keep a high discount rate on equities. With the Fed having hiked 25 bps on September 16 per week-ahead previews, long yields are testing how much valuation the market can carry.

### 4. Bank earnings and CPI headline the week
**Source:** Reuters and other week-ahead previews
**So what:** JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup and Wells Fargo are expected Tuesday, with others after; previews disagree on a few dates. September CPI is expected Wednesday, October 14, with forecasts of about 3.6% year over year headline. A hot print could revive bets on another hike at the October 27-28 meeting.
