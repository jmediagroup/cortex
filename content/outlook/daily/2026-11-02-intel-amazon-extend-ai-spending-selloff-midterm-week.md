## Headlines & analysis

### 1. Intel posts one of its worst days on record
**Source:** Reuters, via The National
**So what:** Intel fell more than 25% (other reports say roughly 28% to 30%) after a soft fourth-quarter revenue outlook, even though it beat on quarterly profit. Reuters also cited a cash-burn warning. Investors are punishing cash consumption in the AI buildout.

### 2. Amazon slides on AI spending plans
**Source:** Reuters; other syndicated reports
**So what:** Amazon fell about 12% as its capex plans and weak trailing free cash flow raised the question of when the spending pays back. One report put the combined Amazon and Intel market-value loss near $200 billion.

### 3. The pattern from Thursday repeats
**Source:** Prior Cortex post; AP
**So what:** Alphabet's record one-day drop on Thursday and now Amazon and Intel on Friday point to the same filter: the market wants visible returns on AI capex and is sorting spenders accordingly.

### 4. Midterms and jobs data loom
**Source:** Reuters Wall St Week Ahead (Oct 30)
**So what:** Tuesday's midterm elections will show whether Democrats take control of Congress, which could mean more regulatory scrutiny of banks and crypto. The October jobs report is due Friday, November 6, with economists expecting roughly 60,000 jobs after September's weak 29,000.
