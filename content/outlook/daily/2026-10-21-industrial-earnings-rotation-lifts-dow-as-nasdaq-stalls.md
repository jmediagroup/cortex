## Top of mind

Stocks rose on Tuesday, October 20, as the Dow gained 1.0% and the S&P 500 added 0.3%, while the Nasdaq Composite finished flat, so the bounce came from earnings in the old economy rather than from AI leadership. 3M jumped more than 5% on a beat and a raised full-year outlook, and a pullback in oil helped rate-sensitive groups. The open question for a long-term plus opportunistic investor is whether this is healthy rotation or just a pause in the AI-capex worry that hit stocks last week.

**Source:** Yahoo Finance, Reuters-based coverage, Oct 20

**Source:** Yahoo Finance and CNBC live coverage, Oct 20

**Source:** CNBC live coverage, Oct 20-21
