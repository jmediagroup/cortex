## Top of mind

The Fed held at 3.75%-4.00% on Wednesday, October 28, in a 10-2 vote with Hammack and Cook dissenting in favor of a hike, and markets now price roughly an 85% chance of a December increase. The major indexes ended slightly higher after swinging through Chair Warsh's press conference. After the close, Microsoft's results landed well while Alphabet's disappointed, so the next test is whether AI spending is rewarded selectively.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. Fed holds, but two officials wanted a hike
**Source:** CNN, Yahoo Finance, Bernama
**So what:** The 10-2 vote shows the committee's debate is about how much more tightening, not about cutting. Warsh stressed price stability and said credit conditions have tightened for some businesses and households. For borrowers and bond holders, the December meeting is now the live event.

### 2. Microsoft beats; Azure grows 48%
**Source:** Motley Fool and other earnings recaps
**So what:** Reported adjusted EPS of $4.89 and revenue near $92.3 billion topped expectations, with Azure revenue of about $33.2 billion. Sources disagree on the after-hours gain (roughly 2% to 4%), so the size of the reaction is unconfirmed.

### 3. Alphabet misses and falls sharply after hours
**Source:** Motley Fool and other earnings recaps
**So what:** Cloud beat, but earnings, total revenue and several ad metrics fell short, and free cash flow turned negative, largely on losses on stakes in other companies. Reported declines range from about 8% to nearly 10%, so treat the magnitude as unsettled until Thursday's regular session.

### 4. Warsh says the Fed will have one fewer meeting next year
**Source:** Bernama
**So what:** A procedural change, but it compresses the decision calendar when inflation is the central worry. It matters less than the dissents.
