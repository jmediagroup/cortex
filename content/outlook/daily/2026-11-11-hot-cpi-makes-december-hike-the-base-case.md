## Headlines & analysis

### 1. October CPI beats on headline and core
**Source:** Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), Seeking Alpha, Benzinga
**So what:** Headline rose 0.4% and core 0.3% month over month, each a tenth above forecast. There is no year-over-year figure this month because October 2025 data is missing from last year's shutdown, so month-over-month is the only clean read, and it ran hot.

### 2. Futures now fully price a December hike, and 50 bps is on the table
**Source:** Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), Benzinga
**So what:** Markets moved from about 84% odds on Monday to fully pricing at least a 25 bp hike at the December 8–9 meeting, per CME FedWatch as cited by Reuters. Fed Governor Waller had reportedly said a core reading of about 0.3% or higher would make him consider a half-point move, and odds of 50 bps briefly neared 15% before easing to about 10%. BlackRock's Rick Rieder framed it as a question of "the extent of the hike."

### 3. Nvidia falls 5.4%, down 9.6% in two sessions
**Source:** Reuters (via Yahoo Finance)
**So what:** Nvidia dragged the tech sector to a 1.4% loss even as peers had rallied on Monday. Coverage ties the slide to an ongoing independent investigation involving its CEO plus scrutiny of AI-infrastructure financing. I have not verified the investigation's details and am not repeating specifics. The signal: index-heavy single names are now a source of headline risk, not just beta.

### 4. CoreWeave beats and raises after the bell
**Source:** Seeking Alpha, Stocktwits, CNBC preview
**So what:** Q3 revenue was reported at $3.66 billion (up 168% year over year) against about $3.55 billion expected, full-year revenue guidance was raised to $13.0–$13.3 billion, and backlog reached about $132.5 billion. After-hours gains were reported anywhere from about 3% to 15%, so the move is unsettled. It matters because CoreWeave is a bellwether for the debt-funded AI build-out the market has been questioning all month.
