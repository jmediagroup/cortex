## Top of mind

Stocks rallied hard on Wednesday, November 4, after Democrats won control of Congress: the Dow gained about 3% to 53,356, its best day since April 2025, and WTI crude fell more than 8% to $88.12. The leadership was uneven, though. AI infrastructure names lagged, so the market looks to be rotating rather than simply risk-on. Thursday's futures were mixed, with Nasdaq-100 futures weaker than the Dow's.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. Democrats take Congress, and the market reads it as lower political risk
**Source:** CNBC, CBS News, CNN
**So what:** Investors appeared relieved by the clear outcome and by the president's acceptance of it. Analysts quoted by CBS expect gridlock to limit big tax and spending packages, which the market seems to welcome. Gridlock also raises the chance of oversight of AI and large tech, which may explain some of the AI-name weakness.

### 2. Oil drops more than 8%
**Source:** CNBC live coverage
**So what:** WTI settled at $88.12. Lower energy costs help consumers and transports, but oil is still high in absolute terms and one report flagged possible renewed U.S. combat operations in Iran as a risk that could reverse the move.

### 3. AI chips and infrastructure diverge from the rally
**Source:** CNN
**So what:** Nvidia fell about 3% and an AI infrastructure ETF about 9% on a day the Dow soared. After last week's Alphabet, Amazon and Intel selloffs, this suggests the AI-capex repricing is continuing even as the broader market recovers.

### 4. AMD and solar stocks jump
**Source:** Market-session recaps (AMD +6.2%, First Solar +8.1%)
**So what:** AMD rose after forecasting faster-than-expected 2027 growth. I could not confirm the details of its guidance from a primary source, so treat that as unverified. First Solar's gain fits a theme of money moving toward energy and policy-sensitive names after the election.
