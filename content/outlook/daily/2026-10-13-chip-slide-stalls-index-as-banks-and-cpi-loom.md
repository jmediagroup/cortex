## Top of mind

The S&P 500 ended Monday, October 12, essentially flat at about 7,807 (-0.06%) as the Dow edged up and the Nasdaq slipped on another memory-chip selloff, with energy leading and technology lagging. A rotation from tech into value is healthy, but it also means the index is being held up by fewer stocks.
