## Top of mind

Alphabet's Class A shares fell 12.6% on Thursday, October 29, their worst day since trading began, wiping out roughly $482 billion in market value on worries about search ad growth and AI spending. The Nasdaq dropped 1.4% and the S&P 500 fell 0.3%, yet most stocks rose, so the damage was concentrated in a few large AI names. Microsoft, which reported the same evening as Alphabet's results, ended roughly flat per one source.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. Alphabet suffers its worst day ever
**Source:** AP, CNN, Yahoo Finance
**So what:** Investors fretted over slowing growth in Google's search advertising alongside a continued ramp-up in AI spending. Reported market value lost ranges from about $482 billion to "roughly half a trillion," depending on outlet. It is the clearest sign yet that the market wants a payoff from AI capex, not just a promise of one.

### 2. Microsoft holds its ground after earnings
**Source:** AP-based market recap
**So what:** One source has Microsoft ending flat while Alphabet dropped more than 12%. After the Azure-led beat reported Wednesday evening, the gap between the two is the story: the market is separating AI spenders by visible returns.

### 3. The index masks the breadth
**Source:** AP closing recap
**So what:** The S&P 500 fell only 0.3% even with a $482 billion hit to one megacap, because most stocks rose. Cap-weighted indexes are carrying concentrated risk in a few names.
