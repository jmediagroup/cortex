## Top of mind

Stocks rose on Wednesday, October 21, with the Nasdaq Composite up about 1.05%, the S&P 500 up 0.58% and the Dow up 0.69%, a reversal from Tuesday's flat Nasdaq as AI-linked names caught a bid ahead of earnings. The after-hours tape was less friendly: IBM swung from a large gain to a loss mid-call, and Tesla's early gains faded. The question for a long-term plus opportunistic investor is whether the market is now paying only for flawless guidance.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. IBM beats, then the stock reverses during the call
**Source:** Motley Fool and Yahoo Finance coverage, Oct 21-22
**So what:** Revenue of about $17.1 billion topped the roughly $16.9 billion consensus, and shares jumped about 7% after hours before sliding to a loss near 4.6% as the call progressed. A beat that cannot hold a gain suggests investors were listening for guidance and AI-demand commentary, not the quarter itself.

### 2. Tesla beats with positive free cash flow; reaction is mixed
**Source:** Yahoo Finance, Oct 21
**So what:** Adjusted EPS of $0.48 on about $28.05 billion in revenue, with better-than-expected capex and positive free cash flow. Reports differ on the extended-hours move (flat-to-slightly-up versus down about 2%), and some commentary flagged cooler data-center spending plans as a worry for AI-supply-chain investors. Treat the first-hour reaction as unsettled.

### 3. AT&T falls almost 6% despite a beat
**Source:** Investor's Business Daily, via secondary coverage, Oct 21
**So what:** Earnings and revenue topped consensus, but subscriber growth looked underwhelming. Defensive, income-oriented names are not immune when the growth metric disappoints.

### 4. SpaceX and Meta keep the AI-capex debate front and center
**Source:** CNBC market coverage, Oct 20-21
**So what:** SpaceX shares rallied after Tuesday's results, and Meta Platforms was among the names listed as big after-hours movers on Wednesday. I could not confirm the size or direction of Meta's move, so I am not drawing a conclusion from it.

## Ideas — opportunistic

### IBM — Post-earnings reversal
- **Catalyst:** A revenue beat followed by a large after-hours swing from gain to loss; Thursday's open and analyst estimate changes set the tone.
- **Time horizon:** Days to a few weeks.
- **What would invalidate:** Analysts broadly raising targets and the stock reclaiming Wednesday's close, or conversely a clean break below the after-hours low.
- **Risk note:** Intraday reversals on calls can extend either way; size small and define the exit first. A watch item, not a call.

### T — Guidance-light selloff
- **Catalyst:** A near 6% drop despite a beat, driven by subscriber growth.
- **Time horizon:** Through the next sessions as estimates reset.
- **What would invalidate:** Further subscriber-growth weakness, or evidence the drop was a one-off positioning unwind.
- **Risk note:** Valuation and dividend questions need checking in the filing; a one-day move is not a thesis.
