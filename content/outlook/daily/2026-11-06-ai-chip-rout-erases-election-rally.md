## Top of mind

Stocks sold off sharply on Thursday, November 5: CNBC reported the S&P 500 down 4.36% to 7,353.73, its worst day since April 2025, with the Nasdaq entering a correction. That reverses Wednesday's post-election rally, and the lagging AI-chip names from that day turned into the leaders of the decline. After a rally that the AI group sat out, the market is now pricing worry that a Democratic Congress could slow data-center buildout.
