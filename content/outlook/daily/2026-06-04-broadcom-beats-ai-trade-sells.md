## Top of mind

Broadcom (AVGO) closed down 12.5% at $419.46 on June 4 — the market punishing CEO Hock Tan's decision to hold the FY2026 AI semiconductor forecast at $56B rather than raise it, even as Q2 beat across every major metric. The Dow hit a fresh all-time record (+1.73%, +875 points to 51,562) while Nasdaq closed essentially flat (−0.09%), producing one of the most bifurcated tapes of the year: AI-adjacent semis sold hard, financials and healthcare bought hard. Brent eased ~3% to ~$97 on conditional ceasefire signals in the Iran-US standoff — but ADP's stronger-than-expected +122K May private payrolls print means tomorrow's NFP (consensus ~80K) could put that macro relief right back on ice.

## Ideas — long-term core

*Quality businesses, durable competitive advantages, reasonable valuation. Hold horizon: years.*

### AVGO — Broadcom
- **Thesis:** Today's selloff is an expectations reset on one specific metric — the unraised guide — not a thesis break. AVGO confirmed AI semiconductor revenue of $10.8B (+143% YoY) and secured multi-year supply agreements with Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta for multi-gigawatt AI compute deployments beginning FY2027. The FY2027 AI semiconductor target of $100B+ is intact, implying ~2.5x growth from the current annual run rate over two years. VMware infrastructure software — generating ~$7B/quarter in recurring revenue — compounds quietly beneath the AI story and provides a durable floor.
- **Valuation note:** At $419.46, AVGO trades at approximately 26–27x forward estimates — below the ~30x premium heading into earnings and meaningfully below Nvidia's ~35x. For a business combining contractual multi-year AI supply relationships with $7B/quarter in recurring software revenue, this is the most attractive entry point since February 2026.
- **Why now (or why patient):** Patient accumulation over the next 3–5 sessions makes more sense than buying the gap. Today's institutional behavior — gap absorbed but no sharp recovery — suggests price discovery is still in progress. The June 5 NFP and any analyst PT revisions will set the near-term support level.
- **Risks / bear case:** The Q3 AI chip guide of $16B vs. the $17.2B analyst expectation could signal hyperscaler AI capex is plateauing earlier than modeled. Google insourcing its own chips — Macquarie's core downgrade thesis — is the most structurally dangerous long-term risk. Oil holding above $95 plus a Warsh hawkish hold on June 17 could compress 26x multiples further before the thesis reasserts.

### UNH — UnitedHealth Group
- **Thesis:** BofA's June 4 upgrade to Buy (PT $450) is supported by improving medical cost ratio (MCR) trends and a favorable Q2 setup. Healthcare as a sector is the rotation destination of record today — defensively valued, insulated from AI multiple compression, and benefiting from the same institutional flows that drove the Dow's all-time record. The upgrade reflects a fundamentally motivated thesis, not just short-term price momentum.
- **Valuation note:** UNH has been under pressure for much of 2025–2026 on elevated MCR concerns. Following today's 5%+ move, the forward P/E is still below its 3-year average — a discount that continues to compress as MCR normalizes toward historical levels.
- **Why now (or why patient):** The rotation trade is real and may have multi-session legs. Healthcare is one of the few large-cap sectors with valuation support AND fresh institutional conviction. This setup is timely, not just watchlist maintenance.
- **Risks / bear case:** UNH faces ongoing DOJ investigation exposure and Medicare Advantage reimbursement rate risk in the 2027 policy cycle. A hot NFP tomorrow that resets rate expectations could temporarily stall the defensive rotation. Any MCR deterioration back above consensus estimates unwinds the upgrade thesis immediately.
