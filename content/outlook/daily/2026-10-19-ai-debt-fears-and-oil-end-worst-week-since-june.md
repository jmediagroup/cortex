## Top of mind

Stocks fell hard on Friday, October 16: the S&P 500 lost 1.1% to 7,659.44 and the Nasdaq Composite lost about 2.1% to 26,140.03, its worst week since June (-4.5%). The trigger was a credit worry, not just a valuation one: debt-laden AI infrastructure names like CoreWeave (-12%, with its bonds yielding above 15%) may struggle to service borrowings if rates stay high, while oil jumped more than 3% on Middle East supply fears. This week's Meta, Tesla and chip earnings are the test of whether the AI trade is resetting or breaking.

## Headlines & analysis

### 1. AI financing fears spread from stocks to bonds
**Source:** Reuters-based wires (via Kitco, Devdiscourse), AP
**So what:** CoreWeave fell 12% and its bonds sold off through the week, pushing yields above 15%. When the market starts pricing the debt of AI builders at junk-style yields, the debate moves from "is demand real?" to "who can fund the buildout?" That hits suppliers' order books and sentiment at the same time.

### 2. Chips post their longest losing streak since 2008
**Source:** Reuters-based wires, AP
**So what:** The PHLX chip index fell 3.8% on Friday for an eighth straight loss. Micron fell 5.6%, Intel 5.8% and Broadcom 4.5%. Eight straight down days is the kind of washout that often precedes a bounce, but it can also mark the start of a re-rating if earnings guidance disappoints.

### 3. Oil jumps again on Middle East supply worries
**Source:** Reuters-based wires
**So what:** Crude rose more than 3% Friday. After September CPI came in at 3.7% annually (0.6% on the month), higher energy prices make the Fed's job harder ahead of its October 27-28 meeting and pressure stock multiples. One strategist noted that high oil tends to weigh on price/earnings multiples.

### 4. Earnings season turns to the AI trade's gatekeepers
**Source:** Reuters, LSEG IBES via market previews
**So what:** Meta, Lam Research, Texas Instruments, Tesla, Netflix, GE Aerospace and Procter & Gamble report this week. S&P 500 third-quarter earnings growth is expected above 30% per LSEG IBES, so the bar for results is high and the market is looking at capex guidance and financing commentary more than the headline beat.

### Semiconductor bounce watch (MU, INTC, AVGO)
- **Catalyst:** Eight straight losses for the chip index, plus Lam Research and Texas Instruments earnings this week as read-throughs on demand.
- **Time horizon:** Days to weeks.
- **What would invalidate:** Another down leg in the chip index after the earnings read-throughs, or weak guidance from Lam or TI.
- **Risk note:** Catching a falling streak is a mean-reversion bet, not a thesis; oversold can stay oversold. Do not treat CoreWeave-style credit stress names as a bounce candidate without looking at the bond market first.
