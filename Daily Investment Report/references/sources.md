# Trusted sources

A short guide to where to look for what. Prefer primary and high-quality secondary sources. Be skeptical of anything that doesn't appear here, especially for individual stock claims.

## Tier 1 — Primary / institutional

| Source | Best for |
|---|---|
| Federal Reserve (federalreserve.gov) | Fed decisions, minutes, speeches, balance sheet |
| US Treasury (treasury.gov) | Yields, auctions, debt issuance |
| BLS (bls.gov) | CPI, NFP, unemployment, JOLTS |
| BEA (bea.gov) | GDP, PCE, personal income |
| SEC EDGAR (sec.gov/edgar) | 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, insider filings |
| Company IR pages | Earnings releases, guidance, transcripts |
| CME FedWatch | Rate-cut probabilities |

## Tier 2 — High-quality financial press

| Source | Strength |
|---|---|
| Reuters | Breaking news, fast and accurate |
| Bloomberg | Markets coverage, terminal-grade data |
| Wall Street Journal | Corporate, M&A, deep dives |
| Financial Times | Global macro, Europe-aware |
| Barron's | Weekend-style analysis, ideas |
| The Economist | Macro framing, weekly perspective |

## Tier 3 — Useful with skepticism

| Source | Use for | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| CNBC | Quick headlines, live tape | TV-driven, often noisy |
| MarketWatch | Quick recaps | Rewrites of wires |
| Seeking Alpha | Author thesis pieces | Quality varies wildly — judge author |
| Yahoo Finance | Data + headlines | Aggregator, check primary |

## Avoid for this report

- Anonymous Twitter/X "calls" without attribution
- Reddit-style hype threads
- Penny stock newsletters and email pump lists
- Pure technical-analysis content with no fundamental grounding
- Anything with affiliate-driven "best stocks to buy now" framing

## Specific data lookups

- **Real-time quotes & indices:** Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, CNBC quote pages
- **Yields:** US Treasury daily yield curve page
- **Volatility:** CBOE VIX page or Yahoo Finance ^VIX
- **Earnings calendar:** Nasdaq earnings calendar, Yahoo Finance, Earnings Whispers
- **Economic calendar:** Investing.com, Bloomberg, BEA/BLS release schedules
- **Analyst ratings:** Look for the originating firm name; treat aggregator-only mentions with skepticism
